# Maharashtra Calendar Experience Research

**Research date:** 17 August 2026  
**Purpose:** Inform an original, offline-first enhancement of Spiritual Companion's festival calendar. This document records public product descriptions and authoritative public-holiday information. It is not a licence to copy a third party's content, wording, artwork, data compilation, or branding.

## Publicly Observable Breadth

| Calendar expectation | Public evidence | Original application direction |
|---|---|---|
| Daily lunar and solar context | Kalnirnay's public site exposes Tithi, Nakshatra, Yog, Karan, sunrise, and sunset in its daily Panchang. [1] | Extend the existing offline Panchang card with a dedicated day-detail calendar sheet that presents data already calculated locally. |

## Legal and Product Boundaries

Spiritual Companion must not copy Kalnirnay's event compilation, language, forecasts, artwork, page structure, paid almanac material, or branding. The original calendar will instead combine: local Panchang calculations already used in the app; independently curated devotional routines; and clearly attributed public holiday information from official public sources. Every religious or timing-oriented suggestion will remain clearly labelled as a personal aid rather than ritual authority.

## Proposed First Release Scope

The initial enriched calendar should add a date-focused calendar surface with a selected-day summary, an accessible month navigator, category chips, and locally available events. It should prioritise 2026 Maharashtra public holidays and existing devotional festival guides, then progressively add broader India observances only when source and date provenance are clear. No GPS, maps, network refresh, provider scraping, or background activity should be required.

## Browser Verification Note

On 17 August 2026, the public Kalnirnay surface was reviewed only to verify high-level expectations: daily Panchang presentation and month-oriented browsing. The MMRDA public-holiday page was separately reviewed to confirm that the 2026 government list contains 24 entries, including Maharashtra-relevant Gudhi Padwa, Maharashtra Din, Ganesh Chaturthi, Dasara, and Diwali dates. These observations support the product scope above; the app must continue to use its own data structures, wording, artwork, and Compose layout.
