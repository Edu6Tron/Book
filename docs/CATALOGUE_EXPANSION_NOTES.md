# Catalogue Expansion Source Notes

This working note records the verified public sources used for the v1.2.1 catalogue increment. It supports the app's offline, address-only directory policy and is not shown inside the app.

| Source | Finding retained for curation | Planned use |
|---|---|---|
| [Himachal Pradesh Language, Art and Culture Department — Temples Trust](https://lac.hp.gov.in/temples-trust/) | The official acquired-temple table names individual public religious institutions and identifies their district under the Himachal Pradesh Hindu Public Religious Institution and Charitable Endowments Act framework. | Add only clearly named acquired-temple records, with the official page retained as each record's source URL. |
| [Tamil Nadu HR&CE temple list](https://hrce.tn.gov.in/hrcehome/temple_list.php) | The Government of Tamil Nadu HR&CE site exposes state and district temple-list routes and identifies the department as the site owner and maintainer. | Retain the department directory as a permitted future source, but do not transcribe dynamic records until the listing is individually confirmed. |
| [Incredible India — Navratri](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/festivals-and-events/navratri) | The Ministry of Tourism's cultural guide describes Navratri as nine nights and notes distinct regional observances, including Garba, Durga Puja, Ramlila, and Kolu. | Keep concise festival guidance regional, optional, and free of asserted local dates. |
| [Ministry of Culture](https://www.indiaculture.gov.in/) | The site identifies the Ministry's mandate as preserving and promoting tangible, intangible, and knowledge heritage. | Use as broad cultural provenance for concise, non-prescriptive offline guidance. |

The app must continue to avoid GPS, maps, location inference, background network work, and copied commercial-directory addresses. Time-sensitive observance dates remain explicitly subject to a locally published Panchang.
